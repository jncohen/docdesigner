1611 Credentials and the Architecture of Advantage

Results

Table 1 reports coefficients from the survey-weighted model of log net worth. Consistent with the threshold hypothesis, the two credential indicators carry most of the explanatory weight, while the continuous years term is small and imprecise once the thresholds are entered.

Interpretation

The estimates describe a step function rather than a ramp. That two credentials absorb most of the structure implies that the wealth returns to education are not smoothly cumulative but are gated at institutional boundaries.3

Where sorting is institutional rather than skill-based, the marginal year of schooling should carry little weight relative to the credential itself—precisely the pattern observed here.

Future work should establish whether these discontinuities extend to flow measures such as saving behavior, and whether they have widened across successive waves of the survey.
