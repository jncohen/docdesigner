Results

Table 1 presents the fitted coefficients. The two credential thresholds carry the bulk of the explanatory weight, while the incremental years-of-schooling term is both small in magnitude and imprecisely estimated once the thresholds are included in the specification.

Read together, the estimates describe a step function rather than a ramp. The magnitude of the bachelor's threshold in particular dwarfs the marginal year, a result that recurs across alternative codings of attainment.

"The credential, not the curriculum, appears to mark the boundary at which the opportunity structure changes. Where sorting is institutional, the marginal year of schooling carries surprisingly little weight." (Delgado 2019, p. 204)

Whether the same discontinuities appear in flow measures such as saving rates, as opposed to the wealth stock analysed here, is a question the panel component of the survey may eventually support.
