Cross-Sectional Differences in Wealth by Education

Measurement and theory alternatives
Joseph N. Cohen
Meridian University

Abstract  This article examines the relationship between educational attainment and household wealth in the United States using the 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances. Net of age, race, and marital status, most detectable structure is captured by two credential benchmarks — high-school and bachelor's-degree completion — while finer gradations contribute comparatively little to model fit.
