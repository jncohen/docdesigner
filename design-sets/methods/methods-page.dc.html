Cohen · Wealth by Education Data & Methods

2Data and methods

The SCF is a nationally representative survey of U.S. household balance sheets that pairs a geographic sample with an oversample of high-wealth households. Because dollar wealth is heavily skewed, we model log(networth) and estimate every quantity on the survey's replicate-weight design so that standard errors reflect the complex sample.

Dummies for high-school (hs), bachelor's (ba), and graduate (grad) completion enter alongside a continuous years-of-schooling term, letting the data adjudicate between the two functional forms directly.
