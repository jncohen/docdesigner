Cross-Sectional Differences in Wealth by Education

Measurement and theory alternatives
Joseph N. Cohen1
1Department of Sociology
June 23, 2026
Abstract

This note examines the empirical relationship between education and household wealth accumulation in the United States using the 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances. Net of age, race, and marital status, most of the detectable structure is captured by two broad benchmarks — high-school and bachelor's-degree completion. Finer distinctions above those thresholds yield comparatively small improvements in model fit.
