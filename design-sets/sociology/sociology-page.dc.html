Results

Table 1 reports coefficients from the survey-weighted model. Consistent with the threshold hypothesis, the two credential indicators carry most of the explanatory weight, while the continuous years term is small and imprecise once thresholds are included.

Discussion

The estimates describe a step function rather than a ramp. That two credentials absorb most of the structure implies the wealth returns to education are not smoothly cumulative but are gated at institutional boundaries.

Where sorting is institutional rather than skill-based, the marginal year of schooling should carry little weight relative to the credential itself — precisely the pattern observed here.

Future work should test whether these discontinuities extend to flow measures such as saving behavior, and whether they have widened across successive survey waves.
