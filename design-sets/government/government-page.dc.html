2.  Findings

Adjusting for age, race, and marital status leaves the credential pattern intact. Two thresholds — completion of high school and completion of a bachelor's degree — account for the bulk of the differences in median net worth, while intermediate steps add comparatively little.

The adjusted estimates in Table 2 narrow the raw gaps but do not close them, indicating that the association is not merely a byproduct of the demographic composition of each attainment group.
