Household Wealth by Educational Attainment, 2022

Estimates from the Survey of Consumer Finances
Prepared by J. N. Cohen, Economic & Demographic Research Division
Highlights
  • Median net worth rises sharply at two credential thresholds — high-school and bachelor's-degree completion.
  • Attainment above a bachelor's degree adds comparatively little to median household wealth.
  • Estimates are net of age, race, and marital status and use the survey's replicate-weight design.

1.  Introduction

This bulletin presents estimates of household net worth by the educational attainment of the household head, drawn from the 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances. It documents a well-known association — that the better educated hold more wealth — and characterizes its functional form.

Estimates are presented both unadjusted and net of demographic controls. All figures reflect the survey's complex sample design; standard errors are computed from replicate weights and account for multiple imputation.
