Threshold structure in the accumulation of household wealth

J. N. Cohen1, A. R. Delgado1,2, P. Menon2 & D. Okafor3
1Department of Sociology, Meridian University. 2Center for Population Research. 3Institute for Economic Analysis.

The relationship between education and household wealth is widely documented, yet its functional form is not. Using the 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances, we test whether wealth returns to schooling are continuous or concentrated at discrete credential thresholds. Net of age, race and marital status, most detectable structure is captured by two benchmarks — high-school and bachelor's-degree completion — while finer distinctions contribute little.
